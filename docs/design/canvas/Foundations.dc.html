One look for every surface: a research instrument, dense and quiet, where identifiers are monospace and absence is a word, never a blank.

Absence vocabulary (Spec section 6)

absentthe record did not carry this member
no runa staged value that never produced a run
not serveda seam that has no party yet (classifier, full map)
uncomputablethe SPU's identity sentinel, drawn as what it is
stuba connector answered by its stub, shown in the header on every surface

A zero, a blank cell and an empty string are values. None of them ever stands in for an absence.
