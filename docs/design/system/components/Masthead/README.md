The masthead is the server's bar on every page, in two rows: on top, brand, the agent switcher, three tabs, who is signed in, the open-alert count and sign out; beneath, the sub-tabs of the page's tab. Under both rows, an availability banner for each plane the server cannot reach.

- The consumer provides the current agent's name (in `id`, inside `.agent-switch`), the pages the person's grants reach, the open-alert count and the person's name and highest role.
- The current page is `.here`: `fg` with an `accent` underline. Every other link is `dim`.
- The alert count is a `.status.fail` pill only when it is above zero; at zero it is not shown. It is a status, not a tab: it stands at the right, after who is signed in, and links to the agents.
- The availability banner (`.unavailable`) is `accent-soft` on a `line` rule and names the agent, the plane's connector and since when, in the words the server uses: "agent unavailable, admin-con down", "turns unavailable, gate-con down".
- **The bar stands in two rows** (the operator's words of 2026-11-13). The top row holds three tabs, in this order: Agents, the agents' work; Agent Operations, which holds The Record and is where experiments, the conversation with an agent, multi-agent coordination and foundation-model support are to stand once the work outside it is largely complete; and IAM, identity and access. A tab opens the first of its sub-tabs the person may open.
- **The second row (`.masthead.tabs`) holds the sub-tabs of the page's tab**, the current one `.here` and the row named by its tab in `aria-label`. Under Agents, the work in the order a person does it, each carrying the stage marks it serves: Declarations, 1 Declare; Register, 2 Provision; Your agents, 3 Run and 4 Observe. Under Agent Operations, The Record, unmarked: it is the record of the whole lifecycle, no stage's own. Under IAM, Persons and Grants for an admin, then Passkeys. A person sees the sub-tabs they may open: Your agents, The Record and Passkeys.
- The second row has no rule of its own: it is a second `.masthead`, the same height, links and `.here`. The design system has no tabs component, and `.rail` is the agent's stage rail, which stands under the bar on the agent's page.
- The marks here state the order of the server's pages. Where one agent stands in its path is the agent's stage rail, not the masthead.
- The served masthead carries no agent switcher and names no agent until multi-agent management; its alert count is `.masthead a.status`, a served-surfaces rule of `bundle.css` that keeps the link's padding and underline off the pill. Two more served rules keep the brand and the sign-out button on one line each, and at phone width (760px and under) let the masthead's row wrap, its links to a second line, rather than run past the page's edge.
