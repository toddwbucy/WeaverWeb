The elections

They derive into the agent's turn-context and, under spu-instruction.decoder, its residual-readout-election and surprisal-election, at WeaverAgent {{ corpus }}. Continuous keeps the session's context accumulating turn by turn; independent answers each turn from the prefix the open seated and its own request alone. The residual readout has no default in the agent's grammar and is required. The surprisal election makes the per-position surprisal exist; unelected, the generation's perplexity stands in its place. A row saved before the screen held them shows the turn context at continuous and the surprisal unchecked, the agent's readings of their absence, and a save writes all three. It derives into the agent's [state-election] at WeaverAgent {{ corpus }}. Every kind crosses to the store with its envelope, or only the kinds named do; each row adds, for its kind, the payload key paths that cross beside the envelope, one dotted path per line from the payload root, at most 32 for a kind and 256 characters each. A kind with no paths is written: with every kind crossing it adds nothing, and with only the kinds named crossing it makes that kind cross with its envelope alone, presence itself being state. The kinds offered are the trace's canonical kinds but save_point, which no election matches; a kind named twice is refused, since the agent would take the first entry for it and drop the rest. Beside the store none, anything but every kind with no rows is refused, since admin refuses an election there, and no table is written. A path is not checked against its kind's payload, so a mistyped one saves and elects nothing, as at the agent; a payload key holding a dot cannot be spelled; and a kind outside the twenty-five cannot be named. A row saved before the screen held the election shows every kind and no rows, the default admin resolves its absence to, and a save beside sqlite writes it.
