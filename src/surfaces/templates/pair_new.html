2 Provision

The two disks

builtokthe operator's rulings of 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24

The appliance comes with no state disk and no models disk. It comes with itself.

The operator adds both in the hypervisor, sized to their need: a models disk at least 10 GB larger than the model, and a state disk of at least 10 GiB.

The operator lays the disks before pairing, with our help. Each is mounted at its default path.

Where a disk already holds data, the appliance's setup script asks at the console whether to keep it or erase it.

The 10 GB margin is guidance. No page judges it.

The operator's rulings of 2026-11-23 and 2026-11-24. The declaration's first page shows the two directories as the appliance reports them.
